RAG AUTOMOTIVE DATASET — MERCEDES-BENZ GROUP AG
Document purpose: source-based automotive knowledge corpus for Retrieval-Augmented Generation experiments.
Data snapshot: 2025 corporate sales/financial data plus selected India-market model information available around August 2026.

IMPORTANT DATA QUALITY RULES
1. Corporate sales, production, wholesale, retail and exports are different measures.
2. Fiscal years differ between manufacturers. Do not compare fiscal-year numbers with calendar-year numbers without checking the period.
3. Model specifications vary by market, model year, trim and powertrain.
4. Prices are India-market snapshots and are not universal global prices.
5. Where an exact figure was not found in the cited source, the field is marked market-dependent rather than invented.
6. This is a curated, paraphrased dataset. It is not a copy of any annual report.


### 1. Company identity and overview

Mercedes-Benz Group AG is the company represented by this document. The document is designed to capture corporate identity, vehicle portfolio, technology, manufacturing, sales geography, financial indicators and model-level information in one retrievable unit.

The main reporting period for the sales dataset is Calendar 2025. The headline vehicle figure is 2,160,000 cars and vans; Mercedes-Benz Cars 1,800,800; Mercedes-Benz Vans 359,100. This headline should always be interpreted together with the reporting definition used by the company.

Automotive companies publish multiple measures. A delivery may refer to a vehicle handed to a customer, a wholesale may represent a vehicle sold through a distribution channel, a retail sale may represent the end-customer transaction, production measures vehicles leaving a manufacturing line, and exports measure vehicles shipped across borders. These measures can differ substantially.

For RAG evaluation, this distinction is intentionally preserved. A strong system should not answer a question about customer deliveries using a production number simply because both numbers contain the word 'vehicles'.

The document also contains selected model information. These model records are deliberately structured so that retrieval can answer questions about body style, engine or electric architecture, output, mileage, dimensions, price and safety/technology themes.

### 5. Engine and powertrain specifications

The powertrain portfolio for Mercedes-Benz Group AG includes the technologies represented in the selected models: 1.3L petrol or market-dependent powertrains; 2.0L diesel mild hybrid in cited Indian comparison; 2.0L petrol mild hybrid in cited comparison; 2.0L petrol or 2.0L diesel; AWD; Petrol/AMG/electric variants depending on market.

Internal-combustion engines can be compared by displacement, cylinder arrangement, forced induction, maximum power, maximum torque, transmission and fuel type. Hybrid systems require additional fields such as battery architecture and electric-motor contribution. Battery-electric vehicles require battery capacity, motor output, charging rate and range.

A RAG dataset should not collapse all powertrain types into one generic 'engine' field. An electric vehicle does not have engine displacement in the same sense as an internal-combustion vehicle. A hybrid may contain both an engine and electric motor.

Transmission values should also be preserved. Manual, automatic torque-converter, dual-clutch, CVT, AMT and single-speed EV reductions represent different technologies and should be retrievable independently.

Power figures are variant-dependent. The selected model records therefore use representative values and explicitly state when the value is variant or market dependent.

### 9. Safety and driver-assistance information

Safety information should be represented using discrete features such as airbags, electronic stability control, anti-lock braking, traction control, cameras, parking sensors, autonomous emergency braking, lane-keeping support, adaptive cruise control and blind-spot monitoring.

Safety equipment is usually trim-dependent. A feature appearing in a premium variant should not automatically be attributed to the base variant.

Safety ratings also depend on market, test protocol and model year. A crash-test result from one market should not be generalized to every market configuration without checking the test scope.

For RAG, safety questions are useful because they require semantic retrieval from feature descriptions rather than only exact numeric matching.


### 10. EV and electrification strategy

Mercedes-Benz Cars delivered 168,800 BEVs in 2025; Vans delivered 28,500 eVans. Mercedes-Benz said eVan sales rose 46%.

Electrification can be represented at several levels: mild hybrid, full hybrid, plug-in hybrid, battery electric and fuel-cell electric. The distinction matters because each technology has different charging, fuel and energy characteristics.

The corporate-level EV section should be connected to model-level records through model names and powertrain metadata. This allows questions such as 'Which selected models are fully electric?' or 'Which models use hybrid systems?'

For regional analysis, the RAG system should retrieve both corporate strategy and actual sales data. A strategy statement is not proof of sales volume. Similarly, a sales number does not by itself explain the company's technology strategy.


### 11. Manufacturing and production

Mercedes-Benz Group AG operates within a global automotive manufacturing and supply ecosystem. Manufacturing information is relevant because vehicle sales, production and exports are connected but not identical.

Useful manufacturing fields include plant location, production capacity, model produced, powertrain type, localization, supplier network, battery production, joint ventures and logistics routes.

A manufacturing plant may produce vehicles for several countries, and a vehicle sold in a region may have been produced in a different region. This makes manufacturing-location questions an excellent test of retrieval precision.

For this dataset, manufacturing claims should be paired with the relevant corporate report or official manufacturing source. If a plant-level fact is not explicitly available in the source set, it should not be invented.


### 12. Technology and software

Modern automotive technology spans powertrain control, connected services, infotainment, advanced driver assistance, digital cockpits, over-the-air software updates, navigation, vehicle-to-cloud communication and increasingly software-defined vehicle architectures.

The selected model portfolio should be enriched with technology fields when a source provides them. Examples include ADAS packages, digital displays, smartphone integration, connected-car services, remote vehicle functions and charging technology.

Technology is another strong RAG category because product names and technical concepts often appear in different sections of a long corporate document. A query may mention a technology without mentioning the model name.


### 13. Financial information

The headline financial context for Mercedes-Benz Group AG is tied to the reporting period Calendar 2025. Mercedes-Benz's 2025 annual report describes cash generation, cost discipline, production capacity planning and the launch of new electric architectures.

Financial values should retain their currency and scale. KRW trillion, Japanese yen billion, euros billion and Indian rupees crore are not directly comparable without normalization.

For cross-company RAG questions, the safest approach is to retrieve the source values first and perform arithmetic only after retrieval. This reduces the chance of mixing fiscal periods or currency units.

Financial information is included because automotive companies are not only manufacturers; they also operate financing, leasing, insurance, technology and other businesses in many cases.


### 14. Warranty and after-sales context

Warranty is a market-specific commercial term. It can cover the vehicle for a fixed period or mileage, while separate warranties may apply to batteries, paint, corrosion and accessories.

The dataset should therefore treat warranty as a structured field with market, duration, mileage limit, battery coverage and exclusions. A warranty advertised in one country should not be assumed to apply globally.

For the current model snapshots, warranty should be verified against the manufacturer's latest India warranty terms before making a customer-facing decision. This file deliberately avoids turning dealer or third-party warranty summaries into universal corporate policy.


### 15. Market share and competitive context

Market share is a ratio and must always specify the market, segment, period and denominator. Passenger-vehicle share, SUV share, EV share and total automotive share are different measures.

The company sales figures in this document are manufacturer-level volumes. They should not be interpreted as market share unless the relevant total market denominator is also available.

Competitive RAG questions can compare two or more manufacturers, but the system should retrieve matching definitions. A global company delivery figure should not be compared with a domestic retail-sales figure without an explicit caveat.


### 16. Historical trend interpretation

The reporting data for Mercedes-Benz Group AG should be used as a time-series corpus when multiple annual observations are available. The most important dimensions are vehicle volume, regional mix, electrified volume, revenue, operating profit and model mix.

A useful RAG question can ask whether sales increased or decreased from one year to another, but arithmetic should be performed from retrieved values rather than from memory.

The same principle applies to regional trends. If two years of regional sales are available, the system can compute absolute change and percentage change. The source period and unit must remain attached to the numbers.
