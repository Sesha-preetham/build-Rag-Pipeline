HYDERABAD TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Hyderabad, Telangana, India
Overview: Historic Deccan city known for Nizami heritage, biryani, technology districts and a blend of old and modern Hyderabad.
Historical note: Hyderabad was founded in 1591 and the Charminar became one of its best-known symbols.
Best months: October to February for more comfortable sightseeing.
Weather: Hot summers; monsoon rain generally arrives during the middle of the year.
Top attractions: Charminar, Golconda Fort, Salar Jung Museum, Hussain Sagar, Chowmahalla Palace, Mecca Masjid and Ramoji Film City.
Local attraction detail: Charminar was built in 1591 and is a central landmark of the old city.
Suggested opening hours: Monuments and museums have individual schedules; verify before travel.
Ticket prices: Charminar area is free to explore; museums, forts and Ramoji Film City charge admission.
Restaurants/Food: Try Hyderabadi biryani, haleem in season, kebabs, mirchi ka salan and Irani chai.
Restaurant areas: Old City, Banjara Hills, Jubilee Hills, Hitech City and Gachibowli.
Hotels: Budget to luxury options are available around Banjara Hills, Hitech City, Gachibowli, Secunderabad and near the airport.
Transportation: Hyderabad Metro, TSRTC buses, taxis, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs.
Airport: Rajiv Gandhi International Airport is roughly 25-30 km from central Hyderabad depending on the destination.
Distance example: Charminar to Golconda Fort is roughly 10-12 km by road.
Distance example: Charminar to Hussain Sagar is roughly 6-8 km by road.
Safety: Use normal urban precautions and expect crowds around major old-city markets.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 2,000-4,500/day; mid-range INR 5,000-10,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 Charminar and old city; Day 2 Golconda and museums; Day 3 Hussain Sagar and modern Hyderabad.
RAG facts: Founded in 1591; Charminar is a central landmark; best months October-February.
