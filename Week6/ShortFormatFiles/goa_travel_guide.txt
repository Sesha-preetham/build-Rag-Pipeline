GOA TRAVEL GUIDE
Region/Country: Goa, India
Overview: India's smallest state by area, famous for beaches, Portuguese-influenced heritage, churches, cuisine and coastal tourism.
Historical note: Goa was ruled by Portugal for centuries before joining India in 1961.
Best months: November to February for beach activities and relatively dry weather.
Weather: Tropical; June to September is the main monsoon period with heavy rain.
Top attractions: Baga Beach, Calangute Beach, Anjuna, Palolem, Old Goa churches, Basilica of Bom Jesus, Dudhsagar Falls and Panaji.
Local attraction detail: Old Goa is known for major churches and colonial-era heritage.
Suggested opening hours: Beaches are open-access; churches, museums and wildlife attractions have individual schedules.
Ticket prices: Beaches are generally free; water sports, museums, guided tours and some attractions are paid.
Restaurants/Food: Try Goan fish curry, vindaloo, xacuti, bebinca and local seafood.
Restaurant areas: Panaji, Candolim, Baga, Calangute, Anjuna, Vagator and Palolem.
Hotels: Budget guesthouses, resorts and luxury hotels are available across North and South Goa.
Transportation: Taxis, rental cars, scooters, buses and airport transfers are commonly used.
Airport: Goa has Manohar International Airport at Mopa and Dabolim Airport serving different parts of the state.
Distance example: Panaji to Calangute is roughly 15 km by road.
Distance example: Panaji to Palolem is roughly 70 km by road.
Safety: Use licensed water-sport operators, avoid swimming in dangerous sea conditions and do not drink and drive.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 3,000-6,000/day; mid-range INR 7,000-15,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 North Goa beaches; Day 2 Old Goa and Panaji; Day 3 South Goa beaches or Dudhsagar excursion.
RAG facts: Portuguese rule ended with Goa joining India in 1961; best months November-February.
