LONDON TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: London, United Kingdom
Overview: Capital of the United Kingdom and a major global centre for history, culture, finance, theatre and museums.
Historical note: London developed from Roman Londinium and later became a major political, commercial and imperial capital.
Best months: May to September for longer daylight and generally milder weather, though rain is possible year-round.
Weather: Temperate oceanic climate; winters are cool and summers are usually mild to warm.
Top attractions: Tower of London, Buckingham Palace, British Museum, Westminster Abbey, Houses of Parliament, London Eye, Tower Bridge and Hyde Park.
Local attraction detail: Many major national museums offer free general admission, while special exhibitions may be paid.
Suggested opening hours: Museums and attractions have individual schedules; advance booking may be required for popular sites.
Ticket prices: Several museums are free; attractions such as the Tower of London and London Eye require paid tickets.
Restaurants/Food: Try fish and chips, Sunday roast, pies and London's highly diverse international food.
Restaurant areas: Soho, Covent Garden, Borough Market, Shoreditch, South Bank and Camden.
Hotels: Budget to luxury options are available across Zones 1-3; good transport access can matter more than staying beside a landmark.
Transportation: London Underground, buses, Overground, Elizabeth line, taxis and walking.
Airport: Heathrow is roughly 25-30 km west of central London by road; rail travel can be quicker than road depending on traffic.
Distance example: Westminster to Buckingham Palace is about 1 km.
Distance example: Trafalgar Square to Covent Garden is about 1 km.
Safety: Use normal big-city precautions, watch belongings in crowded transport and use licensed transport.
Budget estimate: Budget GBP 120-220/day; mid-range GBP 250-450/day excluding major shopping.
3-day idea: Day 1 Westminster and South Bank; Day 2 British Museum and Covent Garden; Day 3 Tower of London and East/Central London.
RAG facts: Roman Londinium; several major museums have free general admission; Heathrow roughly 25-30 km from central London by road.
