SINGAPORE TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Singapore
Overview: A compact island city-state known for efficient transport, multicultural food, modern architecture, gardens and strict civic rules.
Historical note: Modern Singapore developed as a British trading port in 1819 and became an independent republic in 1965.
Best months: Singapore is warm year-round; travel can be planned throughout the year, with rainfall possible in every season.
Weather: Tropical, hot and humid, with frequent showers and thunderstorms.
Top attractions: Marina Bay, Gardens by the Bay, Merlion Park, Sentosa, Singapore Zoo, Chinatown, Little India and Kampong Gelam.
Local attraction detail: Marina Bay combines the Merlion area, skyline views and major attractions.
Suggested opening hours: Outdoor areas are generally accessible; attractions have individual schedules and timed-entry rules.
Ticket prices: Many parks and public areas are free; major paid attractions can be expensive and prices vary by date and package.
Restaurants/Food: Try hawker-centre dishes such as chicken rice, laksa, satay, kaya toast and chilli crab.
Restaurant areas: Chinatown, Little India, Kampong Gelam, Clarke Quay and Orchard Road.
Hotels: Budget, mid-range and luxury stays are available around Bugis, Chinatown, Orchard, Marina Bay and the airport.
Transportation: MRT, public buses, taxis, ride-hailing and walking; public transport is efficient and widely used.
Airport: Changi Airport is roughly 20 km from Marina Bay by road.
Distance example: Marina Bay to Gardens by the Bay is about 1-2 km depending on the exact points.
Distance example: Changi Airport to central Marina Bay is roughly 20 km.
Safety: Generally regarded as a safe destination; obey local laws and avoid prohibited behaviour.
Budget estimate: Budget SGD 100-180/day; mid-range SGD 220-400/day excluding major shopping.
3-day idea: Day 1 Marina Bay; Day 2 Chinatown/Little India/Kampong Gelam; Day 3 Sentosa or wildlife attractions.
RAG facts: British trading port from 1819; independent republic from 1965; Changi to Marina Bay roughly 20 km.
