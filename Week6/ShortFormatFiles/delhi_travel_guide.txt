DELHI TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Delhi, India
Overview: India's national capital territory with Mughal, Sultanate, colonial and modern heritage.
Historical note: Delhi has been associated with several historic cities and empires, making it one of South Asia's major heritage destinations.
Best months: October to March; winter sightseeing is popular, although fog and air quality can affect travel.
Weather: Very hot summers, monsoon rain from roughly July to September and cool winters.
Top attractions: Red Fort, India Gate, Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb, Lotus Temple, Akshardham and Jama Masjid.
Local attraction detail: Qutub Minar and Humayun's Tomb are major UNESCO-recognized heritage sites.
Suggested opening hours: Each monument has its own schedule and weekly closure rules; verify before visiting.
Ticket prices: Many major monuments charge entry; prices can differ for Indian and foreign visitors.
Restaurants/Food: Try chaat, parathas, kebabs, butter chicken, chole bhature and Old Delhi sweets.
Restaurant areas: Connaught Place, Old Delhi, Khan Market, Hauz Khas and Aerocity.
Hotels: Budget to luxury options are available in Paharganj, Connaught Place, Karol Bagh, Aerocity and South Delhi.
Transportation: Delhi Metro, buses, taxis, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs.
Airport: Indira Gandhi International Airport is roughly 15-20 km from central Delhi depending on the destination.
Distance example: India Gate to Connaught Place is roughly 2-3 km.
Distance example: Central Delhi to Qutub Minar is roughly 15-20 km depending on the route.
Safety: Avoid unofficial taxi offers, keep valuables secure and use extra caution late at night in unfamiliar areas.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 2,500-5,000/day; mid-range INR 6,000-12,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 central Delhi; Day 2 Old Delhi; Day 3 Qutub Minar, Humayun's Tomb and South Delhi.
RAG facts: National capital; major Mughal and colonial heritage; best months October-March.
