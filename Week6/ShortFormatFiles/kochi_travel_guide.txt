KOCHI TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Kochi (Cochin), Kerala, India
Overview: Historic port city on India's southwest coast known for maritime trade, backwaters, colonial architecture and multicultural heritage.
Historical note: Kochi became an important spice-trade centre influenced by Indian, Arab, Portuguese, Dutch and British connections.
Best months: October to March for relatively comfortable sightseeing.
Weather: Tropical and humid; southwest monsoon brings heavy rain roughly from June onward.
Top attractions: Fort Kochi, Chinese Fishing Nets, Mattancherry Palace, Jew Town, Paradesi Synagogue area, Marine Drive and Kerala backwater excursions.
Local attraction detail: Fort Kochi is known for colonial streets and waterfront views.
Suggested opening hours: Outdoor waterfront areas are flexible; museums and heritage buildings have individual schedules.
Ticket prices: Walking Fort Kochi is largely free; museums, palaces and boat trips may charge admission.
Restaurants/Food: Try appam and stew, seafood, Kerala meals, puttu, fish curry and local spices.
Restaurant areas: Fort Kochi, Mattancherry, Panampilly Nagar, Marine Drive and Kakkanad.
Hotels: Heritage stays are popular in Fort Kochi; business hotels are common in Ernakulam and Kakkanad.
Transportation: Ferries, Kochi Metro, buses, taxis, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs.
Airport: Cochin International Airport is roughly 35-45 km from Fort Kochi depending on route and traffic.
Distance example: Fort Kochi to Mattancherry is roughly 3-5 km.
Distance example: Fort Kochi to Ernakulam city centre is roughly 10-15 km depending on the route.
Safety: Use standard city precautions; monsoon conditions can affect ferry and road travel.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 2,500-5,000/day; mid-range INR 6,000-12,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 Fort Kochi; Day 2 Mattancherry and Jew Town; Day 3 backwater or island excursion.
RAG facts: Historic spice-trade port; strong Portuguese, Dutch and British heritage; best months October-March.
