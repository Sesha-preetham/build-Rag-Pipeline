CHENNAI TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Chennai, Tamil Nadu, India
Overview: Coastal capital on the Bay of Bengal and a major centre for Tamil culture, business, education and classical arts.
Historical note: The British East India Company established a settlement here in 1639; Fort St. George became an important colonial landmark.
Best months: November to February for relatively cooler sightseeing weather.
Weather: Hot and humid for much of the year; June to September can bring southwest-monsoon rain and October to December can bring northeast-monsoon rain.
Top attractions: Marina Beach, Fort St. George, Kapaleeshwarar Temple, Government Museum, San Thome Basilica, Valluvar Kottam and Besant Nagar Beach.
Local attraction detail: Marina Beach is about 12 km long and is popular around sunrise and in the evening.
Suggested opening hours: Beaches are open-access areas; museums, forts and temples have their own schedules and may close on specific days.
Ticket prices: Marina Beach is free; paid attractions vary, so verify current official prices before travel.
Restaurants/Food: Try South Indian breakfast, filter coffee, idli, dosa, pongal, Chettinad dishes and seafood.
Restaurant areas: Mylapore, T. Nagar, Nungambakkam, Anna Nagar and Besant Nagar.
Hotels: Budget, business and luxury hotels are available near T. Nagar, Egmore, Nungambakkam, OMR and the airport corridor.
Transportation: Chennai Metro, MTC buses, suburban rail, taxis, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs.
Airport: Chennai International Airport serves domestic and international routes.
Distance example: Airport to central Chennai is roughly 15-20 km depending on the destination.
Distance example: Chennai to Mahabalipuram is about 55-60 km by road.
Safety: Use licensed/app-based transport, protect valuables in crowded places and be cautious near the sea.
Budget estimate: Budget traveller INR 2,000-4,000/day; mid-range INR 5,000-10,000/day excluding major shopping.
3-day idea: Day 1 Marina Beach and Fort St. George; Day 2 Mylapore and museums; Day 3 Besant Nagar or Mahabalipuram day trip.
RAG facts: Coastal city; Bay of Bengal; Marina Beach about 12 km; best months November-February.
