DUBAI TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Dubai, United Arab Emirates
Overview: Major Gulf city known for modern skyscrapers, luxury tourism, shopping, desert experiences and historic trading districts.
Historical note: Dubai developed from a small trading and pearling settlement into a global business and tourism centre, especially after oil-era growth and diversification.
Best months: November to March for more comfortable outdoor sightseeing.
Weather: Very hot summers; winters are warm and more suitable for outdoor activities.
Top attractions: Burj Khalifa, Dubai Mall, Dubai Fountain area, Palm Jumeirah, Burj Al Arab views, Dubai Marina, Al Fahidi Historical District and desert safaris.
Local attraction detail: Burj Khalifa is one of Dubai's most famous landmarks and observation-deck visits use timed tickets.
Suggested opening hours: Major attractions have individual schedules; malls generally stay open late.
Ticket prices: Public beaches can be free or low cost; major attractions and desert tours are paid and prices vary by package and time.
Restaurants/Food: Wide international choice; try Emirati dishes, Arabic grills, shawarma, mezze and global cuisine.
Restaurant areas: Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, JBR, Deira, Al Seef and DIFC.
Hotels: Budget to ultra-luxury options are available in Deira, Downtown, Marina, JBR, Business Bay and near the airport.
Transportation: Dubai Metro, trams, buses, taxis and ride-hailing; Nol cards are used across major public transport.
Airport: Dubai International Airport is roughly 15 km from Downtown Dubai by road.
Distance example: Downtown Dubai to Dubai Marina is roughly 20-25 km by road.
Distance example: Dubai Airport to Palm Jumeirah is roughly 30 km by road.
Safety: Generally considered safe; respect local laws, dress expectations in appropriate places and summer heat precautions.
Budget estimate: Budget AED 300-600/day; mid-range AED 800-1,500/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 Downtown; Day 2 Old Dubai and Marina; Day 3 Palm Jumeirah or desert safari.
RAG facts: Best months November-March; airport to Downtown roughly 15 km; major public transport includes Metro and trams.
