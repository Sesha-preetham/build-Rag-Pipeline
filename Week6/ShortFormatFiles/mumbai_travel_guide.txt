MUMBAI TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Mumbai, Maharashtra, India
Overview: India's financial and entertainment capital on the Arabian Sea, combining colonial heritage, beaches, business and Bollywood culture.
Historical note: Mumbai developed from a group of seven islands and became a major port and commercial centre.
Best months: October to February for relatively pleasant sightseeing weather.
Weather: Hot and humid in summer; June to September is monsoon season with heavy rain.
Top attractions: Gateway of India, Marine Drive, Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Terminus, Elephanta Caves, Siddhivinayak Temple, Haji Ali and Juhu Beach.
Local attraction detail: Gateway of India is a major waterfront landmark near Colaba.
Suggested opening hours: Outdoor promenades are generally accessible; monuments, museums and caves have individual schedules.
Ticket prices: Marine Drive and Gateway viewing are free; Elephanta ferry and cave access are paid.
Restaurants/Food: Try vada pav, pav bhaji, bhel puri, seafood, Parsi food and Maharashtrian dishes.
Restaurant areas: Colaba, Fort, Bandra, Lower Parel, Juhu and Kala Ghoda.
Hotels: Budget to luxury stays are available in Colaba, Fort, Bandra, Andheri and near the airport.
Transportation: Mumbai local trains, Metro, buses, taxis, auto-rickshaws in suburbs and app-based cabs.
Airport: Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj International Airport is roughly 30 km from CST/CSMT according to district tourism information.
Distance example: Gateway of India to Marine Drive is about 4-5 km by road.
Distance example: South Mumbai to Juhu can take substantial time because of traffic.
Safety: Crowded trains and markets require extra attention to belongings; monsoon travel may face flooding and delays.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 3,000-6,000/day; mid-range INR 8,000-15,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 Colaba and Marine Drive; Day 2 heritage Mumbai; Day 3 Elephanta Caves or Bandra/Juhu.
RAG facts: Arabian Sea; former seven islands; best months October-February; airport roughly 30 km from CST/CSMT.
