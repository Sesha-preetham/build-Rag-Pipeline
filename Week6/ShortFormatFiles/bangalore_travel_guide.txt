BANGALORE TRAVEL GUIDE
City/Country: Bengaluru (Bangalore), Karnataka, India
Overview: Major technology and startup hub known for parks, gardens, food, breweries and a relatively moderate climate.
Historical note: Bengaluru was founded by Kempegowda in 1537 and later developed under several kingdoms and British administration.
Best months: October to February for pleasant weather and outdoor sightseeing.
Weather: Usually milder than many Indian metros; April-May can be warm and monsoon periods bring rain.
Top attractions: Bengaluru Palace, Lalbagh Botanical Garden, Cubbon Park, Vidhana Soudha, Tipu Sultan's Summer Palace, Bull Temple and Church Street.
Nearby attractions: Nandi Hills, Mysuru, Ramanagara and Shivanasamudra are popular excursions.
Suggested opening hours: Parks and monuments have different schedules; verify before visiting.
Ticket prices: Parks may have low entry fees; palaces and museums generally charge admission.
Restaurants/Food: Try masala dosa, benne dosa, idli-vada, filter coffee, Karnataka meals and diverse international cuisine.
Restaurant areas: Indiranagar, Koramangala, MG Road, Church Street, Brigade Road and Jayanagar.
Hotels: Budget to luxury options are common around MG Road, Indiranagar, Koramangala, Whitefield and the airport corridor.
Transportation: Namma Metro, BMTC buses, taxis, auto-rickshaws and app-based cabs.
Airport: Kempegowda International Airport is about 35-45 km from central areas depending on the destination.
Distance example: Bengaluru to Mysuru is roughly 145 km by road.
Distance example: Bengaluru to Nandi Hills is roughly 60 km from central Bengaluru.
Safety: Traffic can be heavy; use marked crossings, trusted transport and normal urban precautions.
Budget estimate: Budget INR 2,500-5,000/day; mid-range INR 6,000-12,000/day.
3-day idea: Day 1 Lalbagh, Cubbon Park and MG Road; Day 2 Palace and heritage sites; Day 3 Nandi Hills or a nearby excursion.
RAG facts: Founded in 1537; major tech hub; airport roughly 35-45 km from central areas.
